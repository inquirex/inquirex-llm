We're a dental group with 45 staff across three offices, and about half of them work from home a couple of days a week. Most of our stuff lives in Microsoft 365 now, but we still have two servers in a closet at the main office that run the imaging software and the shared drives. We're opening a fourth office next year. When something breaks we need a person on it within four hours, not "sometime tomorrow".
