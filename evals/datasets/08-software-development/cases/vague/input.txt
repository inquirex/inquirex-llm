We have an idea for an app that lets neighbors share tools: people list what they have in their garage, and others borrow it for a few days. Phones first, probably. Everyone would sign up with their own account so we know who has what, and borrowers would put down a small deposit through the app in case something comes back broken. We haven't designed anything at all yet. Budget is tight, so we'd want to start small and see where it goes.
