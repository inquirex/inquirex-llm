Okay, the brief. We're building a dog-walking marketplace and the heart of it is a phone app, iPhone and Android both, where owners book walks and walkers get paid. Walkers need it to keep working in parks with no signal and sync when they're back in range, owners want a push alert when the walk starts and ends, and we show the walker's live position on a map during the walk. Everyone signs in with their own account, and owners pay by card inside the app. Behind that there's a web admin panel for our ops team with dashboards on bookings and revenue, and separate permissions for support staff versus admins. We also need a simple public marketing website in front of all this: a blog for SEO and a contact form that captures walker applications as leads. On the plumbing side we talk to exactly three outside services (Stripe, HubSpot and Mapbox), we push every new signup into HubSpot automatically, and partner kennels get webhooks when a booking touches them. Our enterprise partners require a SOC 2 report before they'll sign, so that's on us too. Design-wise we have Figma wireframes but nothing polished. And the timing is painful: we promised investors a launch in eight weeks, which I know is aggressive. After launch we want a team that sticks around for the long haul, not a handoff.
