I'm not even sure this is the right place to start, so bear with me. We got engaged over the summer, and since then the plan has changed roughly once a week. Some days it's a big family thing with every cousin I have; other days we want to run off to the mountains, just the two of us and a witness, and tell everyone afterwards. My partner leans toward the second, I lean toward the first, and our mothers have opinions about both.

Timing is just as unsettled. We've talked about next fall, then the spring after, then "whenever the venue we haven't picked yet is free." The only certainty is that it won't be here: we live in Phoenix, and we both want somewhere with real seasons and maybe snow, so everyone, you included, would be traveling.

What I do know is how I want it to feel. I grew up with my grandparents' wedding photos, the grainy black-and-white ones, and I want something that will age like that. We definitely want a short film too, not just stills, because my partner's grandfather can't travel and would want to hear the vows. And someday I'd love a real printed album, the heavy kind you pass down, rather than a link that expires. Beyond that I genuinely don't know what we need, which is part of why I'm writing to you.
